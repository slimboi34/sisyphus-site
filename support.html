Support

Questions, bugs, or a privacy request? Open a request on our support tracker and we'll reply there. Please don't include health details in a public request; say you need a private reply and we'll arrange one.

Common questions

How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

How do I stop Health access? Health app → Sharing → Apps → Sisyphus.

The watch says "Open the phone app". Open Sisyphus on your iPhone once; today's session is sent to the watch automatically.

A coach reply is wrong. Press and hold it and choose Report reply. A person will review it.

Can I use it offline? Yes. Today's session is cached, and sessions you log offline upload when you're back online.
