Privacy policy

This policy covers the Sisyphus iPhone and Apple Watch apps and the Sisyphus service they connect to ("we"). We collect only what the app needs to write and adjust your training program. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

What we collect

  • Account: the anonymous user identifier from Sign in with Apple and, if you choose to share them, your name and email (which may be an Apple private-relay address).
  • Assessment answers: age, biological sex, height, weight, training history, goals, equipment and sport access, under-developed areas, any injuries you describe, and your physical test results.
  • Training: your generated program and the sessions and sets you log, including from Apple Watch.
  • Apple Health (only if you allow it): daily summaries of resting heart rate, average heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep duration and VO2 max, used to detect low recovery. The watch app saves workouts, heart rate and active energy to Health on your device.
  • Coach messages: what you write to the coach and its replies.

How it is used

To generate and adjust your program, show your history, answer coach questions, and keep the service secure. Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with the AI provider.

AI coach and Google Gemini

Coach replies are generated by Google's Gemini API. Only after you agree in the app, each message is sent to Google together with the recent conversation and your training profile (age, sex, training history, goals, equipment, assessment scores and current program). Your name, email and Apple Health data are not sent. Messages that mention injury, illness or disordered eating, and any reply you report, are flagged for review by a person on the Sisyphus team. Google processes this data under its API terms.

Service providers

Our servers and database are hosted by Railway (United States). Sign in is handled by Apple. AI replies are generated by Google. These providers process data only to run the service.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our servers and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. Data already in Apple Health stays in Apple Health under your control.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain; third-party tokens are encrypted at rest on the server.

Children

Sisyphus is not intended for anyone under 14 and the assessment does not accept younger ages.

Your rights and contact

You can access your data in the app and delete it at any time. For any other privacy request, use the support page. If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app.
